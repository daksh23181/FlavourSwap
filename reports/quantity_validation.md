## Source
- 222,705 of 231,637 recipes have raw ingredient lines (Food.com "recipes with search terms" dump, joined on recipe id).
- 2,080,428 non-empty ingredient lines.

| line status | lines | share |
|---|---|---|
| ok | 1,626,786 | 78.2% |
| no_usda | 212,666 | 10.2% |
| no_amount | 129,541 | 6.2% |
| unconverted | 92,895 | 4.5% |
| unmatched | 18,540 | 0.9% |

`ok` = converted to grams with the ingredient's USDA household measures; `no_amount` = no quantity given (e.g. "salt to taste", counted as 0 g - Food.com's own calculator cannot count it either); `no_usda` = ingredient without a reliable nutrient link; `unconverted` = unit not available for that food; `unmatched` = line could not be tied to an ingredient name.
